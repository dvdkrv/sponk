01 The Pitch

“Balatro meets Candy Crush meets Plants vs. Zombies — with the soul of Thermopylae.”

You command 300 Spartans arranged in a tactical grid. Slide them to form matching groups — when they connect, they charge from the phalanx and strike the Persian line. The horde keeps coming, turn after turn, advancing in patterns dictated by their unit type. Every night you huddle at the bonfire, choose an upgrade, and steel yourself for the next dawn.

At the end of the run, the Persian God King stands waiting. He is a damage check. He is a high‑score. He is the only thing between you and a legend.

02 Three Pillars

  • Match the Phalanx

    Drag Spartans across the grid. Line up matching units and they surge out of formation, smashing into the enemy on the same turn.

  • Permadeath, Always

    Lose a Spartan and he is gone — not just for the fight, but for the entire run. The phalanx only ever gets smaller.

  • Roguelike Forging

    Each night at the bonfire, choose upgrades that reshape your run — elite spears, oracle blessings, cursed relics. No two stands are the same.

03 How It Plays

The Phalanx Grid

The left side of the battlefield is your matrix of Spartans. Slide a soldier to form groups of three or more of the same unit. On match, they charge — the bigger the chain, the harder they hit.

  • Hoplites — reliable midline damage
  • Spear-bearers — pierce through enemy ranks
  • Shield-bearers — soak counterattacks
  • Captains — wildcards that match anything

A live look at the match-3 phalanx.

The Persian Advance

Right‑to‑left, turn after turn, the empire comes. Each unit follows its own movement pattern — archers hang back, immortals march in lockstep, war elephants trample two ranks at once. Read the formation. Plan your matches.

  • Telegraphed attacks every turn
  • Unit‑specific advance patterns
  • Elite enemies with deadly auras

The Bonfire

When night falls, the fighting stops. Gather around the fire. Pick from three offerings — a relic, a blessing, or a curse with teeth. Bury your dead. Sharpen your spears. Tomorrow they come again.

  • Three offerings per night
  • Synergistic builds — bleed, fury, oracle, bronze
  • Optional cursed relics for high‑risk runs

Choose well. The dead are watching.

The God King

At the end of every run, he is waiting. The Persian God King is not a fight — he is a damage check. Whatever you have left of your phalanx, throw it at him in a single, escalating exchange. Survive, and your score is written in bronze.

  • Pure damage‑race finale
  • Global leaderboards every run
  • Daily challenge seed for the brave

05 Frequently Asked

Is this really like Balatro and Candy Crush had a child?

Yes, and that child grew up in Sparta. The board mechanics are tile‑matching, but the meta-progression and build variety lean roguelike deckbuilder.

What happens when I lose all 300 Spartans?

The run ends — even before reaching the God King. Your high score reflects how far you marched.
